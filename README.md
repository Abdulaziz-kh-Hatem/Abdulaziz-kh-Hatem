## 🔬 Academic Profile & Research Focus

I am an honors Biomedical Engineering graduate (**B.Eng., Cumulative Grade: 92.0%, GPA: 4.0 / 4.0, Class Rank: 3rd / Top 7.5%**) from the University of Science and Technology, Aden. My academic trajectory integrates **Analog Front-End (AFE) circuit design**, **physiological digital signal processing (DSP)**, and **interpretable Machine Learning for healthcare telemetry**.

I am a published first author with competitive institutional research grant funding. My senior capstone thesis—evaluating an integrated ocular biopotential interface for assistive typing and robotic wheelchair control—received a **100% Distinction with Highest Honors**. My overarching objective is pursuing a research-intensive **Master's and Ph.D.** focusing on robust biosignal conditioning, wearable cardiovascular telemetry, and reliable clinical health AI.

### Core Research Areas:
- **Biopotential Acquisition & AFE Instrumentation:** Discrete instrumentation amplifiers (AD620), active analog filtering, impedance matching, and high-gain ($\approx 20,000\times$) low-noise analog conditioning for microvolt-level electrooculography and electrocardiography.
- **Physiological Digital Signal Processing:** Baseline wander attenuation, zero-phase digital filtering, Discrete Wavelet Transforms (DWT), moving average techniques, and quantitative Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) evaluation.
- **Cardiovascular & Neuro-Telemetry:** Synchronized multi-modal acquisition (ECG & PPG), Pulse Transit Time (PTT) modeling, optical photoplethysmography physics, and saccadic eye movement tracking.
- **Machine Learning for Health Data:** Supervised diagnostic classification using patient-wise validation to mitigate data leakage, morphology feature extraction, and model interpretability.
